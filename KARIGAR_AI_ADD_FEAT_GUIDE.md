# KarigarAI: Craft to Commerce

## Part 1: User Guide

Welcome to the **KarigarAI: Craft to Commerce** platform! This guide will walk you through how to use the web application, whether you are an artisan looking to digitize your craft or a buyer looking to support local talent.

### For Artisans (Sellers)

The artisan experience is designed to be completely frictionless, removing technical barriers so you can focus on your craft.

**1. Getting Started**
* **Sign Up / Log In**: Create your artisan account. You can use the provided demo accounts for testing.
* **The Dashboard**: Once logged in, you'll see your command center. Here you can track your Total Views, Earnings, Orders, and Active Products at a glance.

**2. Creating a Product (The Hero Feature)**
This is where the magic happens. You don't need to write long descriptions or figure out tags.
* **Snap**: Click on "Create Product" and upload or take a photo of your handmade item.
* **Speak**: Use voice (in Hindi or your regional language) or text to briefly describe what you've made.
* **AI Generation**: Watch the AI process your inputs. It will automatically generate a catchy title, a detailed description, appropriate categories, search tags, and a multilingual listing.
* **AI Image Studio**: The AI will enhance your product photo—removing background clutter, fixing lighting, and making it ready for a marketplace.
* **Smart Pricing**: Review the AI-suggested price, which calculates material, labor, craft complexity, and market signals. You can edit this final price if needed.
* **Publish**: Review the generated listing and click publish!

**3. Your Digital Storefront & Story**
* **My Story**: Record your story using your voice. The AI transforms this into a beautiful "Meet the Artisan" section on your profile.
* **Storefront**: Your public store is automatically created. Use the **Share Store** or **QR Code** feature to send your shop link to customers on WhatsApp or social media.

**4. The AI Business Coach**
* Check the **AI Business Coach** tab on your sidebar.
* It acts as your personal advisor, giving you actionable insights on what products are performing best, suggesting pricing tweaks, and even recommending what designs you should craft next based on market trends!

### For Buyers

**1. Discovering Crafts**
* On the homepage, browse through various categories of authentic Indian crafts.
* Use the search and filter options to find exactly what you are looking for.

**2. Exploring Products & Artisans**
* Click on any product to read its unique AI-generated story.
* Read the "Artisan Story" to learn about the creator behind the craft.
* Use the **Ask the Artisan** feature for direct, multilingual communication.

**3. Ordering**
* Add items to your cart and proceed to checkout.
* You can track the progress of your order in real-time (Pending → Confirmed → Packed → Shipped → Delivered).

## Part 2: The Purpose of this Web App

While this web app functions as a standalone prototype ("Craft to Commerce"), its strategic purpose goes far beyond being just a hackathon MVP. 

**This application serves as a testing ground and visionary blueprint for our main, ongoing project: Karigar AI.**

As our primary Karigar AI platform evolves, this app acts as the vanguard for experimental, highly advanced AI features. It allows us to build, visualize, and polish next-generation capabilities in an isolated environment before we seamlessly merge them into the main product pipeline.

### Future Features Demonstrated Here (To be introduced in Karigar AI):

* **Zero-Friction "Snap & Speak" Onboarding**: Transitioning from traditional form-filling to an entirely multimodal product creation flow where voice and a single photo do 100% of the heavy lifting.
* **Automated Smart Pricing**: The AI's ability to calculate a fair, profitable price based on abstract variables like "Craft Complexity" and "Labor" rather than just fixed material costs.
* **Generative AI Image Studio**: Built-in background removal and lighting enhancement, ensuring that even low-end smartphone photos look like professional studio shots.
* **The AI Business Coach**: Moving beyond static analytics charts to an active, conversational AI that tells the artisan *what to do next* (e.g., "Make more terracotta pots, demand is up").
* **Automated Storytelling**: Using AI to bridge the emotional gap between buyer and seller by turning basic regional-language voice notes into compelling, multilingual brand stories.

By building these out perfectly in this web app first, we ensure that when they are eventually introduced to the main Karigar AI product, they are fully refined, tested, and ready to redefine the digital artisan economy.
