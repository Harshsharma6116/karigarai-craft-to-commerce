# KarigarAI: Craft to Commerce

Build a premium, highly polished hackathon MVP called “KarigarAI” — an AI-powered digital business companion for Indian artisans.

CORE VISION:

Help an artisan turn their handmade craft into a digital business without needing technical skills.

Flow: SNAP → SPEAK → AI UNDERSTANDS → GENERATES → REVIEW → PUBLISH → SELL → GROW.

DESIGN — VERY IMPORTANT:

Make the website visually impressive and premium, not like a basic dashboard or CRUD app.

Use a modern fusion of Indian craftsmanship + futuristic AI.

Dark charcoal/black base with warm terracotta, copper and subtle golden accents.

Use elegant typography, glassmorphism, soft 3D depth, floating cards, subtle shadows, smooth gradients, animated flowing 3D waves/particles and a beautiful glowing AI orb.

Use tasteful Indian craft-inspired textures/patterns in the background.

Add smooth page transitions, hover lift/tilt effects and micro-interactions.

Keep the design clean, premium and professional—not overly colorful or childish.

Fully responsive.

LANDING PAGE:

Brand: KARIGARAI

Tagline: “From Craft to Commerce, Powered by AI.”

Show a beautiful hero visual demonstrating:

Photo → Voice → AI → Digital Store → Customer.

Primary CTA: “Start Creating”

Secondary CTA: “Explore Artisans”

Do not show unnecessary “try demo/test” buttons.

AUTHENTICATION:

Create separate Artisan and Buyer experiences with clean login/signup screens.

Provide demo accounts for testing.

ARTISAN DASHBOARD:

Create a premium business dashboard showing:

• Total Product Views

• Total Earnings

• Total Orders

• Interested Buyers

• Active Products

• Average Rating

• Revenue/Views charts

• Top-performing products

• Recent orders

• AI Business Insights

• “What should I make next?” recommendation

SIDEBAR:

Dashboard

Create Product

My Products

Orders

My Store

Analytics

AI Business Coach

My Story

Settings

CREATE PRODUCT — HERO FEATURE:

Allow an artisan to upload/take a product photo and describe it through voice or text in Hindi/regional language.

AI should generate:

• Product title

• Description

• Category/subcategory

• Search tags

• Craft/style information

• Multilingual listing

• Suggested price + explanation

• Product story

Show an impressive AI processing animation with steps:

Understanding Craft → Identifying Product → Creating Listing → Enhancing Image → Estimating Price.

AI IMAGE STUDIO:

Show Original vs AI Enhanced image.

Include background removal, lighting improvement, crop/cleanup and marketplace-ready image.

Add optional AI lifestyle preview and clearly label generated previews.

SMART PRICING:

Show suggested price and range with:

Material Cost

Labour

Craft Complexity

Market Signals

Estimated Margin

Include “Why this price?” explanation and allow artisan to edit final price.

ARTISAN STORY:

Turn the artisan’s voice into a beautiful “Meet the Artisan” story section with craft, experience, location and audio playback.

DIGITAL STOREFRONT:

Automatically create a beautiful public store for every artisan with:

Products, prices, ratings, artisan story, craft information.

Add Share Store and QR Code.

BUYER EXPERIENCE:

Browse/search/filter artisan products.

Product details should show product story, artisan story and “Ask the Artisan”.

Support multilingual buyer-artisan communication.

ORDERS:

Working demo flow:

Pending → Confirmed → Packed → Shipped → Delivered.

Update dashboard earnings/orders when relevant.

ANALYTICS:

Interactive charts for views, orders, earnings, engagement and product performance.

AI BUSINESS COACH:

Create an AI assistant that provides actionable insights:

• Best-performing products

• Pricing suggestions

• Market opportunities

• Customer interest

• What product/design the artisan should create next

IMPORTANT:

Make the prototype genuinely interactive, not just static screens.

Navigation, forms, upload flow, filters, product creation, AI results, pricing, storefront, QR/share, orders, analytics and AI interactions should work using realistic demo/mock data where external APIs are unavailable.

Use reusable components and clean architecture.

Prioritize a spectacular end-to-end demo experience and polished UI over unnecessary backend complexity.

The final product should feel like a real premium AI startup helping Indian artisans—not a generic e-commerce template.       Make the first impression exceptional. When a judge opens KarigarAI, the visual identity, 3D depth, animations and artisan-focused storytelling should immediately communicate: “This is an AI product built specifically for Indian artisans.”
